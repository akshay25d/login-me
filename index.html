About Us

Akshay Santra Common Services Centre

Authorized CSC & Cyber Cafe — Cycafe

Welcome to Cycafe — an authorised Common Services Centre (CSC) and cyber café operated by Akshay Santra. We are dedicated to providing reliable, affordable digital services to our community.

Our centre offers a wide range of government & digital services including:

  • PAN Card application & correction
  • Aadhar update & enrollment
  • Online form fill-up (government & private)
  • Document scanning, printing & lamination
  • Passport photo printing
  • IRCTC ticket booking
  • Insurance & banking services
  • Internet browsing & digital literacy

Our mission is to bridge the digital divide and bring government services closer to every citizen.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information such as name, mobile number, email address, and document details only as required to deliver the requested services (e.g. PAN, Aadhar, form fill-up). We do not collect information beyond what is necessary.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used solely to process your service requests, maintain transaction records, and provide customer support. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties except as required by law or government portals.

3. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data. Customer records stored digitally are secured via Firebase Authentication and Firestore security rules. Physical documents are handled with care and returned or securely destroyed after use.

4. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us at info@cycafe.in. We will respond within 7 working days.

5. Cookies & Analytics

This portal may use browser local storage to remember your login session. No tracking cookies or third-party advertising cookies are used.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with a revised "last updated" date.
